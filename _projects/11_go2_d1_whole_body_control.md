A **teleoperated** demonstration of a Unitree Go2 quadruped with a D1 arm picking up a cup from the floor and placing it on top of a box. This showcases the robustness of the whole-body controller under teleoperation, coordinating base and arm motion together, and is **not** an autonomous pick-and-place capability.
