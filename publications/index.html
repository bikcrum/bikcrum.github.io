  1. ICRA
    Multi-Quadruped Cooperative Object Transport: Learning Decentralized Pinch-Lift-Move
    Bikram Pandit, Aayam Kumar Shrestha, and Alan Fern
    International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib Code Website

    We study decentralized cooperative transport using teams of N quadruped robots with arms that must pinch, lift, and move ungraspable objects through physical contact alone. Unlike prior work that relies on rigid mechanical coupling between robots and objects, we address the more challenging setting where mechanically independent robots must coordinate through contact forces alone without any communication or centralized control. Our approach enables coordination through shared policy parameters and implicit synchronization cues, scaling to arbitrary team sizes without retraining.

2025

  1. CoRL
    Oral
    Non-conflicting Energy Minimization in Reinforcement Learning based Robot Control
    Skand Peri, Akhil Perincherry*, Bikram Pandit*, and Stefan Lee
    Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), 2025
    Abs arXiv Bib Code Website

    Efficient robot control often requires balancing task performance with energy expenditure. We propose a hyperparameter-free gradient optimization method to minimize energy expenditure without conflicting with task performance, applying policy gradient projection between task and energy objectives. We demonstrate a reduction of 64% energy usage while maintaining comparable task performance, with sim-to-real validation on a Unitree Go2 quadruped.

2024

  1. CoRL
    Spotlight
    Learning Decentralized Multi-Biped Control for Payload Transport
    Bikram Pandit, Ashutosh Gupta, Mohitvishnu S. Gadde, Addison Johnson, Aayam Kumar Shrestha, Helei Duan, Jeremy Dao, and Alan Fern
    Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), 2024
    Abs arXiv Bib Code Website

    We replace wheels with multiple bipedal robots attached to a carrier for payload transport on rough terrain. Our main contribution is a decentralized controller that works with varying numbers and configurations of robots without retraining, trained via reinforcement learning in simulation and transferred to the real world, with results shown on systems of two and three Cassie robots.

  2. ICRA
    Best Paper Finalist
    Learning Vision-Based Bipedal Locomotion for Challenging Terrain
    Helei Duan, Bikram Pandit, Mohitvishnu S. Gadde, Bart van Marum, Jeremy Dao, Chanho Kim, and Alan Fern
    International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2024
    Abs arXiv Bib

    Reinforcement learning for bipedal locomotion has demonstrated robust gaits over moderate terrains using proprioceptive sensing, but blind controllers fail in environments requiring visual perception. We propose a fully-learned system that enables bipedal robots to react to local terrain while maintaining travel speed and direction, achieving successful sim-to-real transfer without explicit pose estimation.

  3. CoRL
    Simple Masked Training Strategies Yield Control Policies That Are Robust to Sensor Failure
    Skand Peri, Bikram Pandit, Chanho Kim, Fuxin Li, and Stefan Lee
    Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), 2024
    Abs Bib HTML

    Sensor failures are common in real-world robot deployments, leading to potential catastrophic outcomes. We propose training reinforcement learning policies that are robust to sensor failures using a multimodal encoder and a training strategy that simulates sensor failures by randomly dropping sensor modalities, evaluated on bipedal locomotion and robotic manipulation.
